How your song is written

We turn the story you already have in your head into something they can press play on.

  1. 01

    Step one

    Tell us the story

    Names, the occasion, a memory that still makes you smile. A short form, as if you were texting a friend.

    No writer's block — we guide you with prompts

  2. 02

    Step two

    We compose it, in your words

    Our system writes lyrics from your story, picks the genre that fits the mood, and produces a full studio master — vocals, instruments, mix.

    Pop, acoustic, rock, soul, folk, hip-hop, jazz — 20+ styles

  3. 03

    Step three

    Press play, together

    Two hours after you press send, the song is in your inbox. MP3 and WAV, ready to play. Share it, save it, send it. Whatever makes the moment bigger.

    Usually delivered in 2 hours · We email you the moment it's ready
