Refund policy

Clear, plain-English. No surprises, no fine print games.

  1. Before production starts (first 2 hours): full refund, no questions asked.
  2. Brief-fit revisions: if the song doesn't reflect your brief — wrong recipient, wrong language, factual errors, wrong occasion — we revise it free for up to 2 rounds.
  3. Materially defective delivery that cannot be fixed in 2 revision rounds: refund minus a 20% production fee.
  4. Subjective dissatisfaction (“I didn't feel it”): not refundable — music is subjective. We offer one courtesy re-generation instead.
  5. Technical failure (corrupt file, non-delivery beyond the promised SLA): full refund or re-delivery, at your choice.

By clicking “Place order” you expressly consent to production starting immediately and waive your 14-day right of withdrawal under EU Directive 2011/83/EU Art. 16(m) and UK Consumer Contracts Regulations reg. 37.

How to request a refund

Email hello@songzy.com with your order email and the reason. We reply within one business day.

Chargebacks

Before raising a chargeback with your card issuer, please contact us. Most situations are resolved within 24 hours by direct message.
